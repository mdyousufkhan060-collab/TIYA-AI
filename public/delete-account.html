Delete your TIYA account

  1. Open TIYA and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → My Account → Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, chats, settings, Workspace data (tasks, notes, business data) and payment requests. Payment records needed by law may be kept for up to 1 year.
