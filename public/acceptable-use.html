ROSE Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. These rules apply to everyone who uses ROSE, and to every store made with the ROSE Store Builder.

Not allowed anywhere

  • Anything illegal in Bangladesh, fraud, scams or fake orders.
  • Sexual content, anything involving minors in a harmful way, hate, harassment or threats.
  • Spam, mass unwanted messages, or using ROSE to call or message people who did not agree to it.
  • Pretending to be another person, shop, bank or brand; fake reviews or fake receipts.
  • Collecting passwords, card numbers or OTP codes.
  • Viruses, hacking, or trying to break ROSE or other users' accounts.

Not allowed to sell in stores

  • Drugs, alcohol where not allowed, tobacco to minors, weapons, explosives.
  • Prescription medicine without a valid licence and prescription process.
  • Stolen, fake or copied branded goods; pirated software, films or books.
  • Gambling, betting, crypto investment schemes, "double your money" offers.
  • Human or animal parts, wildlife products, or anything else banned by law.

Store owner duties

  • Show true prices, delivery charges and a clear return / refund policy.
  • Deliver what was sold, or refund the customer.
  • Use customer data only for that order and support.

What happens if rules are broken

We may hide products, pause the store, or close the account, and report illegal activity to the authorities. To report a store or a user, write to info@myrosebd.com with the link.
