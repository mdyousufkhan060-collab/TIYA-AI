TIYA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Developer: MD Yousuf Khan, Bangladesh. Contact: md.yousuf.khan060@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account: your Google email, name and profile picture (Google sign-in). We never see or store your Google password.
  • Age (birth year or date of birth) to keep content safe for your age.
  • Your chosen mode (Personal, Student or Business) and the data you add in Workspace (tasks, notes, routines, business profile, products, customers, orders).
  • Chats with TIYA, if chat history is turned on, and voice audio only while you are talking to TIYA.
  • Payment requests you submit (method, sender number, transaction ID) for Premium.
  • Basic login activity (time, device type) for security.

How we use it

Only to run TIYA: answer you, remember your settings, show your Workspace and keep your account safe. We do not sell your data. Business customer data is used only for that business.

Who processes it

Supabase (database and login), Google Gemini and Groq (AI replies and speech), Vercel (hosting). Your messages are sent to these AI services only to create a reply.

Permissions

Microphone is used only when you start voice. Other permissions (notifications, contacts, files) are asked only when you use a feature that needs them, and you can refuse.

Children

Users under 18 get age-safe replies and cannot use Business mode.

Delete your data

Inside the app: Settings → My Account → Delete account (removes your account and all your data at once). Or see delete-account page.
