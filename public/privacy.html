ROSE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Developer: MD YOUSUF KHAN, Bangladesh. Contact: md.yousuf.khan060@gmail.com · info@myrosebd.com

What we collect

  • Account: your Google email, name and profile picture (Google sign-in). We never see your Google password.
  • Age (birth year or date of birth) to keep content safe for your age.
  • Your mode (Personal, Student or Business) and what you add in Workspace (tasks, notes, routines, business profile, products, customers, orders).
  • Chats with ROSE if chat history is on, and voice audio only while you talk to ROSE.
  • Payment requests you submit (method, sender number, transaction ID).
  • Basic login activity (time, device type) for security.

Store Builder data

  • Store owners and staff: Gmail, plan, store settings, products, photos, staff list and roles, courier keys (kept locked, never shown back in full).
  • Store customers: when you order from a store made with ROSE, the store keeps your name, mobile, address, division, district, upazila, optional email, order and payment method. The store owner decides how this is used; ROSE stores it for them and uses it only to run the store.
  • Your order details are sent to the courier the store chooses (for example Steadfast) only to deliver the parcel.

How we use it

Only to run ROSE and the stores: answer you, remember settings, show your Workspace and orders, deliver parcels, prevent fake orders and keep accounts safe. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for outside advertising.

Who processes it

Supabase (database, login, file storage), Google Gemini and Groq (AI replies and speech), Vercel (hosting), Hostinger (domain and email), and the courier a store chooses. Messages go to the AI services only to create a reply.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. Paused stores are kept for 30 days and may then be deleted. Deleted accounts are removed from the live database at once; backups clear within 30 days.

Permissions

Microphone is used only when you start voice. Other permissions (notifications, overlay, contacts, files) are asked only when you use a feature that needs them, and you can refuse.

Children

ROSE is not for children under 13. Users under 18 get age-safe replies and cannot use Business mode or open a store.

Your choices

You can see and change your data in the app, turn chat history and memory off (Settings → Privacy Controls), and ask for a copy or correction by email. Store customers can ask the store owner, or write to info@myrosebd.com.

Delete your data

In the app: Settings → My Account → Delete account (removes your account and all your data). Or see the delete-account page.
