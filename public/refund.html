ROSE Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This covers payments made to ROSE. Orders placed in a store made with ROSE are refunded by that store owner, not by ROSE.

1. Payment taken, nothing added

If you paid by bKash, Nagad or Rocket but credits or a plan were not added, send the transaction ID within 7 days (REPORT A PROBLEM in the app, or info@myrosebd.com). We will add it or refund the full amount.

2. ROSE Premium credits

Unused Premium can be refunded within 7 days of payment if no more than 10% of the credits were used. Used credits and expired packages are not refundable.

3. Store Builder plans

  • The 10-day trial is free, so there is nothing to refund.
  • A paid plan can be refunded within 7 days of payment, minus the days already used, if the store did not take real orders in that time.
  • After 7 days, or after cancelling mid-plan, the store stays open until the plan ends; the rest is not refunded.
  • Stores paused or removed for breaking the Acceptable Use Policy are not refunded.

4. Domains

Domain registration fees are paid to the registry and cannot be refunded once the domain is registered.

5. App stores

Purchases through Google Play or the Microsoft Store are refunded under that store's rules; ask them first.

6. How refunds are paid

To the same bKash, Nagad or Rocket number, within 7 working days after approval.
